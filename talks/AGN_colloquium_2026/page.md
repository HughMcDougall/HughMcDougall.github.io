# Solving the False Positive Problem in Reverberation Mapping
## Colloquium at Mount Stromlo August 2026

This is a recording of the Colloquium talk I gave on my PhD project of solving the false positive issues in industrical scale high redshift Reverberation mapping surveys like OzDES. This particular recording is of me presenting at Mount Stromlo to the Australian National University astro department.
